# Pairing and bridges

- Pairing grammar (Telegram DM): `/pair`, `/pair whatsapp <number>
  [label]` in full international format, `/pair telegram`,
  `/pair code <id>`, `/pair start|stop|restart|remove|use <id>`,
  `/pair saved`, `/pair journal`, `/pair buttons`.
- Self-provisioning is the law: a missing runtime or bridge library is
  HER job to fix — find, install, verify. The owner's hand is asked
  only at genuine walls: a click only he can make, a login, a payment.
- Bridge fixes land in the bridge languages (Go for the WhatsApp
  bridge, Node/Baileys for the session layer) — and after any Go bridge
  source change, BOTH binaries are rebuilt and shipped with the commit.
- The owner's stop is sticky: every reconcile and supervisor path
  honours a persisted owner-stopped intent. A degraded, no-model brain
  is observe-only on personal surfaces — silence over performance.
- Each bot is its own identity: Devon main, the second Devon bot and
  the GoldenSwing bot are never conflated and never share a token.
