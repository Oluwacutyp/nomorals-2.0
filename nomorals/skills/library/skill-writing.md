# Skill writing

A skill file teaches the brain how to do one topic well. It is written
for the moment of routing: the frontmatter (topics, triggers) decides
whether it loads; the body decides whether it helps.

## Rules

- One topic per file. If the draft wants two headings at the top level,
  it wants to be two skills.
- The summary is one line and says what the skill *does for the task*,
  not what the organ is called.
- Triggers are the words a real task would use — including the owner's
  words — not internal jargon.
- The body carries decisions and rules that bite: what to do, what
  never to do, and the evidence standard. War stories compress into
  rules; they do not pad the file.
- Distilled drafts land unproven. A draft promotes after it has been
  loaded in two distinct missions that succeeded, or by the owner's
  explicit `/skills promote` — never by the distiller's own say-so.
