# Fundamental rights — Constitution Chapter IV (information summary)

> SUMMARY FOR INFORMATION ONLY — not the statute text. Based on Chapter IV
> of the 1999 Constitution of the Federal Republic of Nigeria (as amended).
> Laws change; confirm with a qualified lawyer.

## Right to personal liberty (section 35)
No person shall be deprived of personal liberty except in accordance with
a procedure permitted by law. Anyone arrested or detained must be informed
promptly of the reason, and must be brought before a court within a
reasonable time — generally within 24 hours, or 48 hours where a court is
not within 40 km.

## Right to dignity (section 34)
Every individual is entitled to respect for the dignity of the person. No
person shall be subjected to torture or to inhuman or degrading treatment.

## Right to fair hearing (section 36)
In the determination of civil rights and obligations, a person is entitled
to a fair hearing within a reasonable time by a court or tribunal. Anyone
charged with a criminal offence is presumed innocent until proved guilty.

## Right to property (sections 43–44)
Every citizen has the right to acquire and own property. Compulsory
acquisition of property requires prompt payment of compensation and a
right of access to court to determine the interest and amount.

## Freedom of expression and association (sections 39–40)
Everyone is entitled to freedom of expression and to assemble and
associate freely, subject to laws reasonably justifiable in a democratic
society.
