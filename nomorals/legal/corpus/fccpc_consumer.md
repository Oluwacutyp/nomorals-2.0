# Consumer protection — FCCPC Act (information summary)

> SUMMARY FOR INFORMATION ONLY — not the statute text. Based on the Federal
> Competition and Consumer Protection Act 2018. Laws change; confirm with a
> qualified lawyer.

## Consumer rights
Consumers have the right to goods and services that are safe and of
acceptable quality, to clear information about what they are buying, and to
seek redress when things go wrong. Defective, fake, or misdescribed goods
violate these rights.

## What to do about a bad purchase
1. Complain to the seller in writing and keep records (receipts, chats,
   photos of the defect).
2. Ask for repair, replacement, or refund within a reasonable time.
3. If the seller refuses, escalate to the Federal Competition and Consumer
   Protection Commission (FCCPC), which receives consumer complaints.

## Misleading conduct
Businesses must not mislead consumers about the nature, quality, or price
of goods and services. Bait advertising and false claims are prohibited.
