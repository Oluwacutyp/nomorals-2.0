# Lagos Tenancy Law — key provisions (information summary)

> SUMMARY FOR INFORMATION ONLY — not the statute text. Based on the Tenancy
> Law of Lagos State 2011. Laws change; confirm with a qualified lawyer.

## Self-help eviction is prohibited
A landlord must not evict a tenant by force, by locking the premises, by
removing the tenant's belongings, or by disconnecting water or electricity.
Eviction must follow due process: proper notice, then a court order for
possession. A tenant locked out or whose utilities were cut off may seek
relief through the courts.

## Notice to quit
Where the tenancy agreement does not state a notice period, the statutory
minimums generally applied in Lagos are: yearly tenant — 3 months' notice;
quarterly tenant — 3 months; monthly tenant — 1 month. Notice must be in
writing and must expire on the anniversary of the tenancy.

## Rent increases
A landlord seeking to increase rent should give the tenant reasonable
notice of the increase. Unreasonable or retaliatory increases can be
challenged. Rent control provisions apply in designated areas.

## Service charge and agency fees
Service charges should reflect actual services rendered. Agency fees in
Lagos are commonly around 10% of annual rent; far higher fees are unusual
and worth questioning.

## Repairs
Structural repairs are ordinarily the landlord's responsibility; the
tenant keeps the premises in good tenantable repair. Agreements should
state who handles what.
